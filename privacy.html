Privacy Policy

Last updated September 11, 2026

In short

  • This site has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking cookies, and no content embedded from other companies.
  • If you use the contact form, your name, email address, and message are sent to me through FormSubmit so I can reply. That is the only reason I collect them.
  • I don't sell, rent, or share your information for marketing.
  • You can ask me to see, correct, or delete your information at any time by emailing diego.crisafu@gmail.com.

1. Who is responsible

This website is the personal portfolio of Diego Crisafulli, an individual based in Montreal, Quebec, Canada. It isn't run by a company and doesn't sell anything.

I'm the person in charge of the protection of personal information for this site. You can reach me at diego.crisafu@gmail.com.

2. What I collect and why

When you use the contact form

Your name, email address, message, and a record that you agreed to this policy. I use them only to read and reply to your message.

When you email me

Your email address and whatever you choose to include. I use them only to reply.

When you visit the site

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs the IP address of every visitor for security purposes, whether or not you're signed in to GitHub. I can't access these logs. GitHub describes this in its GitHub Pages documentation and its General Privacy Statement.

I don't use your information for marketing, profiling, or automated decisions, and I don't combine it with information from other sources.

3. What I don't collect

  • No analytics or visitor statistics.
  • No advertising, social media pixels, or tracking cookies.
  • No videos, maps, or widgets embedded from other companies.
  • No third-party fonts. The fonts are served from this site, so loading a page doesn't send your IP address to Google or any other font service.

4. Your consent

By checking the consent box and sending the contact form, you agree to the collection and use described in this policy. You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing me. I'll then delete your messages, unless the law requires me to keep them.

5. Service providers and transfers outside Quebec

I rely on these providers to run the site and receive messages:

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), operated by Devro LABS, forwards contact form submissions to my email and sends you an automatic confirmation email. Its privacy policy says it processes submissions only to provide its service and doesn't disclose them to third parties unless that's needed to provide the service or required by law.
  • Google (Gmail) stores the emails I receive, including form submissions. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub hosts the site and keeps the security logs described above.

These providers may store or process information outside Quebec and Canada, including in the United States. There, it may be subject to local laws, including lawful access by government authorities.

6. How long I keep it

I keep messages only as long as I need them to reply and follow up. I delete contact form messages and related emails no later than 24 months after our last exchange, or sooner if you ask.

7. Your rights

You can:

  • ask whether I hold personal information about you, and get a copy in a commonly used format;
  • ask me to correct information that's inaccurate, incomplete, or out of date;
  • ask me to delete your information or stop using it;
  • withdraw your consent at any time.

Email diego.crisafu@gmail.com. I'll reply within 30 days, free of charge. To protect your information, I may ask you to confirm your identity, for example by writing from the address you used to contact me.

These rights apply to every visitor, wherever you live. If you're not satisfied with my response, you can file a complaint with the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

8. Security

The site is served over HTTPS, and the contact form is sent over an encrypted connection. I take reasonable steps to protect the messages I receive, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If an incident involving your information creates a risk of serious injury, I'll notify you and, where required, the Commission d'accès à l'information.

9. Children

This site is meant for recruiters and professional contacts and isn't directed at children. If you're under 14, please don't use the contact form.

10. Storage on your device

This site doesn't set cookies. It may store your theme preference and an offline copy of its own files in your browser. The Cookie Policy explains both.

11. Changes to this policy

If I change this policy, I'll update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects how I use information you've already sent me, I'll ask for your consent first.

12. Contact

For questions or requests about your personal information, email diego.crisafu@gmail.com.
