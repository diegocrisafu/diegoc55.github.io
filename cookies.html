Cookie Policy

Last updated September 11, 2026

In short

  • This site doesn't use cookies.
  • It has no analytics, no advertising, and no content from other companies that could set cookies.
  • That's why there's no cookie banner.

What the site stores in your browser

The site uses two browser storage features. Neither is a cookie, neither identifies you, and nothing in them is sent to me or anyone else.

theme

Type
Local storage
Purpose
Remembers whether you chose light or dark mode.
When it's created
Only when you click the dark mode button on the portfolio page.
How long it stays
Until you clear your browser's site data.

portfolio- plus a version number

Type
Cache storage, used by the site's service worker
Purpose
Keeps a copy of the site's own files, such as pages, styles, fonts, and the résumé, so they load faster and work offline.
When it's created
When you first open the portfolio page, if your browser supports it.
How long it stays
Replaced when the site updates, and removed when you clear your browser's site data.

Third parties

  • Every font, image, style sheet, and script loads from this site. No request goes to Google Fonts, analytics providers, or advertisers.
  • GitHub, which hosts the site, logs IP addresses on its servers for security. That happens on GitHub's side and doesn't use cookies. The Privacy Policy has details.
  • The contact form sends your message to FormSubmit only when you submit it, and the request is made without cookies.

How to clear it

You can delete this storage at any time in your browser's settings, usually under "Site data" or "Cookies and site data". Clearing it resets the theme to your system setting and removes the offline copy.

If this changes

If I ever add analytics or other tools that use cookies or track visits, I'll update this policy first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
